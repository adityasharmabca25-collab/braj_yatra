Essential Tips for a Smooth Pilgrimage

🍲

What to Eat in Braj

  • Mathura Peda Dense, caramelized khoya sweet dusted with cardamom and boora sugar. Buy freshly made boxes from century-old shops near Chowk Bazaar and Holi Gate.
  • Kachori-Sabzi & Crisp Jalebi The quintessential Braj morning breakfast. Crisp lentil kachoris broken into peppery potato gravy, followed by hot saffron jalebis.
  • Bedmi Puri with Pumpkin & Aloo Sabzi Urad dal stuffed whole wheat puris served with tangy fenugreek-flavored pumpkin (sitaphal) curry and spiced potatoes.
  • Thick Churned Lassi in a Kulhad Rich, cold curd lassi topped with thick malai (cream) and rabri, served in an earthen clay cup.
  • Strict Sattvic Rule in Vrindavan All authentic eateries in Vrindavan strictly prepare food without onion or garlic. Delicious prasadam thalis are available at affordable prices.
💡

Before You Go (Crucial Tips)

  • Temple Timings Pause in the Afternoon Almost all major temples close between 12:30 PM and 4:30 PM for Thakurji's rest (Shayan). Schedule indoor museums or resting during these hours.
  • Monkey Alert in Vrindavan & Barsana Monkeys are adept at snatching spectacles, sunglasses, and phones. Keep glasses inside bags and avoid using phones in crowded temple alleys.
  • Carry Soft Cotton Socks You must walk barefoot inside temple compounds and parikrama paths. In summer and sunny afternoons, white cotton socks protect your feet from heated marble.
  • Strict Security at Janmabhoomi Krishna Janmabhoomi does not permit phones, smartwatches, bags, or pens inside. Use the dedicated cloakroom lockers before entering queue lines.
  • Senior Citizen Accessibility Barsana provides a ropeway service up to Radha Rani temple. Govardhan Parikrama offers 4-seater battery-operated golf carts at Dan Ghati.
